          Methods

          Network

          A hand-curated signed, directed graph of human proteins and complexes and regulatory interactions across seven canonical cancer pathways. It was assembled from KEGG maps () and the review literature. Each edge records direction, sign (activation or inhibition) and mechanism. Edges that stand for several steps (e.g. PI3K → PIP3 → AKT) are marked indirect. CDKN2A appears twice, as p16INK4a and p14ARF, because its two products act in different pathways. mTOR appears as its two complexes, which respond to drugs differently.

          Centrality

          Betweenness (Brandes' algorithm, directed, normalised) measures how often a node lies on shortest paths between other nodes. High-betweenness nodes act as bottlenecks that carry signal between pathways. PageRank (d = 0.85) ranks nodes by how much upstream regulation flows into them.

          Perturbation propagation

          Perturbed nodes are clamped to −1 (inhibited) or +1 (activated). Every other node relaxes iteratively towards

          xv ← (1−λ)·xv + λ · δ · tanh( γ · Σu→v suv xu / √kvin )

          where suv = ±1 is the edge sign, γ = 1.2 is the gain, δ = 0.85 is the per-hop attenuation and λ = 0.5 is the damping. Because the update is bounded and damped, it reaches a steady state even with feedback loops in the network. Values are changes relative to the unperturbed baseline. The model is qualitative: it predicts the direction and relative size of a change, not concentrations or timing. The guided scenarios are unit-tested against the behaviour reported in the literature.

          Paths & loops

          Breadth-first search lists every shortest path between two proteins. A path's net sign is the product of its edge signs, so two inhibitions in a row act as activation. Feedback loops are elementary directed cycles of up to 8 steps, found by depth-first search and rooted at their lowest-index node so each cycle is counted once.

          Limitations

          The network leaves out many proteins, isoforms, cell-type context, dosage and timing. Treat predictions as hypotheses to test.
